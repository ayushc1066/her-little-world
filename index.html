01 ♡

To Aruu,

If you're reading this, then I guess I'm finally getting to say some of the things I never really know how to say properly when we're talking.

Aruu, you became such a strange and beautiful part of my life. Somewhere between the good morning messages, your random pictures, your voice notes, the little kisses you send, the way you call me baby, and all those completely random conversations, you became someone I started looking for in almost everything.

I know I overthink a lot.

Sometimes I ask for too much, sometimes I need reassurance, and sometimes I turn something small into something much bigger inside my head. I know that can be tiring, and I've genuinely been trying to understand that about myself.

But underneath all of that, there has always been something very simple.

I just care about you.

I care about your day, your mood, your little problems, the things that make you happy, and even the random things you probably don't think are important.

I remember the small things because they're the things that made you feel real to me.

The good morning kisses.
The random photos.
The times you stayed and tried to make my mood better.
The little things you did without making a big deal about them.

Even that video you made for me meant much more than just a video. It made me feel like, somewhere in your day, you actually thought about me.

And maybe that's why I started making things for you too.

The letters.
The gifts.
The little websites.

All those things were basically my way of saying what I couldn't always say directly.

I know our relationship hasn't been perfect.

We've had misunderstandings, bad days, overthinking, jealousy, and moments where we both probably wondered why something so simple had become so complicated.

But even after all of that, I still don't want a perfect relationship.

I just want a healthy one.

Something simple.

Something where we can talk honestly.

And if you're reading this sometime far in the future, I hope you remember that the version of me who wrote this genuinely cared about you with everything he knew how to give.

Maybe I've changed by then.
Maybe you've changed too.

But I hope we never forget the little things that made us choose each other in the first place.

And if nothing else...

I hope you smile while reading this.

Because that's honestly what I wanted every time I made something for you.

To give you one tiny reason to smile.

From Ayush, to his Aruu. ♡
